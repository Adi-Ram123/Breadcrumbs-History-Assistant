5. The diagnostics toggle (for the demo)

The "⋯" menu holds Sync history now and a Show diagnostics switch. It stays on until you turn it off. When it's on, every card gets one extra line (LLM or plain, total time). That line opens the raw Intent with its real field names and the timing breakdown. When it's off, cards look like frames 1–4. Citations are now numbered in row order, and the 10:47 and 10:52 ticks merge into one mark labeled "1, 2".

What these need from the backend (goes into the spec)

1

Counts for "nothing" answers

When nothing matches, /ask also returns how many entries fell in the time window with no topic filter (214 pages, 38 pages). The no-match sentence needs that number to say "you did browse, just not about ramen".

2

Where history starts

/health (or /ask) reports the oldest stored visit, so the panel can say "Your history here starts on Tue, Aug 4". It can also tell a question about dates before that from a real no-match.

3

Results before the sentence

Today /ask returns everything at once. Frame 3 needs the Intent and rows first, then the answer. Two ways to get there: stream /ask as two events, or split it into /ask (Intent and rows) plus /answer. Stop then just drops the second part. This is a real API change and the main design question for the spec.

4

A reason for the plain answer

A field such as fallback_reason: "llm_off" | "stopped" | "rejected_citation" | "llm_error", so the grey line in frame 4 can say why.
