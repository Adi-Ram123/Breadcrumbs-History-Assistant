3. All results open, with long titles

"7 more results" opens the uncited rows (4–10, with grey numbers) inside the same card, and "Show fewer" folds them again. Long titles, emoji and Japanese text stay on one line with an ellipsis. A long question wraps inside its bubble, which is capped at 85% width.

Rules this sets for the spec

1

Scroll anchoring

When you send a question, it scrolls to the top of the thread. If you've scrolled up while an answer is being written, new content never pulls you down. The "Latest answer" button shows instead.

2

What folds

Only the newest card shows its rows. Older cards fold their rows, and only on the next question, never while you're reading. Answer sentences never fold. One diagnostics panel is open at a time.

3

Row cap

Cited rows always show, up to 3. If the answer cites more than 3 (rare with 1–2 sentences), the extras sit behind "more" with the rest. With no citations (a plain answer that cites nothing, or LLM off), the top 3 results show.

4

Spacing

32px between exchanges, 16px between a question and its answer, 16px of padding inside a card. The gap is what tells exchanges apart, so no dividers are needed.
